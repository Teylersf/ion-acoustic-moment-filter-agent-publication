# Density-convention addendum to the independent moment review

The final journal PDF, p.20, Eqs. (B5c), (B5d) and (B6c), was checked directly in a 150-dpi rendering and against the publisher's mathematical strings. Let L denote the off-diagonal temperature divergence and d the in-plane velocity divergence. The printed cross term in (B5c) is 2 u_z L without an additional density factor. Equation (B5d) contains e E_z = L + m u_z d.

The earlier scratch note wrote an arbitrary-density compatibility identity that would need a consistent pressure-versus-temperature convention or an additional density factor to follow in that form. That identity is not asserted as a literal consequence of the source as printed. No source symbol or prefactor has been silently repaired, and no separate quantitative error is inferred from this notation issue.

The retained exact elimination is restricted to the explicitly normalized moment subsystem n=m=e=1. It gives H+(T_zz-u_z^2)d=0, with H the zz heat-flux divergence. The two existing rational witnesses use precisely that normalization. They satisfy the literal (B5c)/(B5d) subsystem and d=0 for distinct positive T_zz values; neither is a full kinetic or fully closed steady-fluid solution.

Independently of this prefactor ambiguity, the displayed (B6c) divides by d. Steady continuity at the stated two-dimensional stagnation point sets d=0; the source also explicitly uses steady uniform-density incompressibility in (B13). Thus the zero-divisor restriction survives without choosing a repair to the density notation. A finite-d limiting path is not presented as a full steady uniform-density solution.
